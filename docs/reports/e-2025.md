## New measurement: held-out families are comparably hard

The honesty check only means something if the held-out shapes are not simply harsher. Measured
on 4000 real eval events (shapes are calibrated on plane area, but candidates cluster at low
`|eta|`, so this had to be measured rather than assumed) -- fraction of real candidates dropped:


The held-out three sit inside the spread of the scoring five at every severity. So a held-out
area below a scoring area means the *shape* failed to generalise, not that the corruption was
stronger. Without this control the held-out number would have been uninterpretable.

Also confirmed: B's generator `FAMILIES` is `("rect","wedge","strip","cells","towers")` -- the
same five as the bench. So ellipse/annulus/diagonal are genuinely out of distribution for both
the training augmentation and the scoring benchmark.
