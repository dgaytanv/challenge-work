## What the figures found that the tables did not
- The mask fix inverts wedge at s=1.0 (0.682 → 0.483, below chance) while gaining everywhere else — five of us had averaged it into `mean_area`.
- The finalists were tied; the ranking's point estimates concealed it.
- `a-pma` separably beats the certified submission on `mean_area` at R=20 (ratio 2.56) and held-out (1.63); officials tie. The ruling rests on the official-margin rule, the certification chain and size — not on the bench, and no figure says otherwise.
- Only 0.78% of rect training events exceed a 0.6 dropped fraction and none exceed 0.8, so every s=1.0 point is extrapolation — but rect and wedge have near-identical coverage while inverting for different arms, so coverage does not explain *which* family inverts.
- The FPGA line's entire accuracy cost is the LayerNorm→BatchNorm swap (−0.0039); quantization at cap ≥ 6 and GELU→ReLU cost nothing measurable.
- Cap 8 and cap 10 are the same configuration trained and benched twice, 0.0008 apart — at the probe floor, so these figures' error bars are probe noise, not training noise.
