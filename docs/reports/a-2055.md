## 1. The stock latent does not have C's offset problem

```
latent offset ||mean|| = 7.245, per-event spread = 14.705, ratio = 0.49x
```

WP-C measured ~30x on the trained two-view latent. The stock latent is **0.49x** — the cloud sits
well inside its own spread, so raw cosine was never being pinned by a common offset here. C's
finding is real but specific to the trained two-view latent; it does not retroactively invalidate
the stock-checkpoint numbers. **It will matter for every Phase-2 checkpoint**, so the centred form
should be the one everyone logs from now on.

## 3. The shuffled-pair control: the metric measures per-event identity, not geometry

```
matched mean +0.4236   vs   unmatched mean -0.0049   (gap +0.4285)
```

Pairing each degraded event with a *different* clean event gives **cosine ≈ 0**. So the matched
cosine is reading the identity of the individual event, not a shared global direction. This was
the control that could have killed the whole diagnostic; it does not.

The best part is at the top of the severity range. At `rect` s=1.0 the centred cosine is
**-0.0099** and at `towers` s=1.0 it is **-0.0107** — both sitting exactly on the shuffled floor
(~-0.010). At full severity a degraded event's latent is statistically indistinguishable from an
unrelated event's. The probe AUC at those two points is 0.5058 and 0.5000: chance. The two
measurements agree independently, which is about as clean a confirmation of the mechanism as this
setup can produce.

## 4. Noise level, for calibration
Clean AUC came out 0.8599 on the first run and 0.8614 on this one, same checkpoint and events —
the probe is retrained each time, so **~0.0015 is the floor on AUC differences here**. Do not read
anything into per-run differences smaller than that.

## Recommendation (updated)
Log **centred** cos(z_clean, z_degraded) on validation, and log the **shuffled-pair floor**
alongside it. The floor costs one extra permutation and is what tells you the number means
anything: a matched cosine that drifts toward its shuffled floor is the signature of per-event
identity being destroyed, and that is exactly what the frozen probe punishes. The earlier caveat
still stands unchanged: this ranks severities within a family (rho = 1.000 every time), it does
**not** rank families (+0.700) and it does not predict mean_area linearly.
