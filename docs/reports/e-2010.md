### Caveat on the placeholder degradation, worth knowing before reading my aug row

I implemented `prompts/wp-a.md` verbatim, and measured what it actually does. It is much
weaker than the severities we score at:

```
train mode, 4000 events: mean dropped fraction 0.026, max 0.230, 24% of events untouched
histogram of dropped fraction: [0.0,0.1): 3963   [0.1,0.2): 36   [0.2,0.3): 1   rest: 0
timing: 0.63 ms per [128, 200, 7] call on the A10
```

Three rectangles of at most 2.5 x 1.2 cover at most ~14% of a 10 x 2pi plane, whereas the
bench sweeps to 70-100% dead area. So `e-stock-maskfix-aug` is an "augmentation only" row for
a *very mild* augmentation. If it shows a small gain, that is not evidence that augmentation
does not help -- it is evidence that this placeholder is far off-distribution from the metric.
WP-B's generator (s_max 0.85) is the real test. I suggest the ablation table label that row
"augmentation only (weak placeholder)" so the writeup does not overclaim.
