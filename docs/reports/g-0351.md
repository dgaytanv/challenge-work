## 3. Plan for G6a / G6b, with what I found in the source before writing any code

**G6b (`io_parallel`, token loop folded) is not a hand-written wrapper — hls4ml already supports it, and it
is one attribute.** Reading the source rather than assuming:
* `hgq.layers.QDense` takes `parallelization_factor` (default −1 → `prod(input_shape[1:-1])`, i.e. fully
  unrolled over tokens, which is what campaign 1 got and why N=400 never compiled).
* `hls4ml/converters/keras_v3/hgq2/_base.py::QDenseHandler` copies that attribute onto the layer config when
  the input rank > 1.
* `hls4ml/model/optimizer/passes/multi_dense.py` rewrites a multidimensional Dense into a `Conv1D` and
  carries `parallelization_factor` across.
* `vivado_backend.init_conv1d` sets `n_partitions = out_width // pf`. With **pf = 1 and out_width = 400 that
  is 400 partitions of one token each — exactly one per-token datapath iterated 400 times**, which is the
  G6b deliverable, and it keeps the arithmetic bit-identical because the weights are shared anyway.
So G6b needs **no retraining and no model change**: same `gF-bits6-relu-l1t` weights, a `parallelization_factor`
sweep (1, 2, 4, …) trading latency against resources. The two layers that do *not* fold are the softmax over
the token axis and the `QEinsum` attention·value combine; between them they are 1.2% of the EBOPs but they
are the part I expect to fight, and I will report what they cost rather than assume.

**G6a (`io_stream`, homogeneous activation quantizers)** requires retraining, because forcing
`homogeneous_axis` to cover the channel axis discards the per-channel activation bit widths. Campaign 2
standard: stage B from stage A (`gA-float-bn-l1t.params.npz`), 6-bit cap, ReLU, L1T train file,
`--eta_max 3.0`, **3 seeds**, benched with `--encoder_class QuantizedPMAEncoder --eta_max 3.0
--train_data robust_tagging_train_data_small_l1t.pt`, compared against `gF-bits6-relu-l1t` (0.8089 ± 0.0005).

**Order.** G6b first (no GPU, no retraining, and it is the route that preserves campaign 1's measured
accuracy by construction), G6a's retraining launched in parallel through `gpu_slot.sh` since it is GPU work
and G6b is CPU work. Synthesis jobs: never more than two at once, `nohup`, logs in `~/c2-g/synth/`.
