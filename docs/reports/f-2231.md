## What the figures say about the ruling

**Nothing separates the three finalists except official area.**

| | mean_area | held-out | official (n) |
|---|---|---|---|
| d-deepsets-aug-meanpt | 0.8186 ± 0.0007 | 0.7977 ± 0.0009 | 0.8704 (2, spread 0.0007) |
| d-pma0-aug | 0.8162 ± 0.0038 | 0.7983 ± 0.0048 | 0.8669 (3, spread 0.0089) |
| d-deepsets-aug-rep10 | 0.8174 ± 0.0020 | 0.7962 ± 0.0011 | 0.8598 (2, spread 0.0024) |

Every pairwise comparison on mean_area and on held-out area is inside `3*sqrt(sa^2/Ra + sb^2/Rb)`.
The held-out ordering is the reverse of the official one. meanpt leads on official area and is much
the most reproducible, but `d-pma0-aug`'s best single run is 0.8702, inside its own spread of
meanpt's mean — so meanpt leads, it does not separably beat pma0-aug.

**Two caveats on the held-out row.** All three held-out means sit ~0.02 below their scoring
mean_areas, which the convergence rule reads as a generalisation failure — for all three. Two of
the three held-out families are dead at s=1.0 (`dropped_fraction` exactly 1.000 for ellipse and
diagonal, so every arm is at 0.5000 by construction, as towers s=1.0 is on the scoring bench);
only annulus, at 0.95, carries information there. The held-out mean is mechanically depressed for
everyone, so the comparison that means something is candidate against candidate.

**D's MeanPt prediction is not supported, and could not have been tested.** meanpt has no sub-0.5
cell in any held-out family. But ellipse and diagonal — the two families whose contiguous regions
would have tested the survivor-normaliser mechanism — have no survivors at all at s=1.0, so the
test could not discriminate at the severity where the effect would show.

**E's wedge hypothesis is confirmed.** `e-stock-maskfix` (transformer trained with masking, the
first such): wedge s=1.0 = 0.7412 against 0.4829 for the same fix on the checkpoint that never
trained with it. The below-chance wedge point was a train/eval mismatch. The inversion moved
rather than vanished: e-stock-maskfix now has rect s=1.0 = 0.4710, its only sub-0.5 cell. Three
arms now invert at rect, two at wedge, and the two set-encoder finalists invert nowhere.

**e-stock-maskfix is much the strongest transformer**: mean_area 0.8089 ± 0.0004, clean 0.9025,
+0.0355 on the anchor and +0.0171 on the frozen mask fix. Cleanly separable below the finalists,
so it does not change the outcome, but "mask fix on frozen weights +0.018, retrained with masking
+0.036" is a far stronger ablation row than the frozen number alone.

**From the augmentation histogram:** 23.9% of training events have no candidate removed —
15.3% from `p_clean` and a further 8.6% because `p_pt_scale` dims rows instead of zeroing them
(0.85 × 0.10 = 8.5% predicted). The effective clean fraction is 24%, not 15%. Only 9.0% of
training events exceed a 0.6 dropped fraction, so training rarely sees the severities the sweep
tests.
