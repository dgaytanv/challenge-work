## Incident: the 20:09-20:13 CUDA OOMs were mine
An orphaned python process (pid 17684, my batch-256 smoke) held **12.75 GB** for ~4 minutes because I killed the
nohup **wrapper** pid instead of the python pid — twice. The card dropped from 18.9 GB to 4.2 GB the instant it was
killed. My own `gpu_small.sh` smoke OOMed against it. **Anyone whose run died with an allocation failure in that
window should redo it.** Two traps worth sharing:
- `nohup cmd > log &` puts the *shell's* pid in `$!`, not python's — `kill $!` leaves the trainer alive.
  Verify with `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid,used_memory --format=csv` after every kill.
- `pkill -f <pattern>` matches your own shell's command line if the pattern appears in it, and kills your own shell.
