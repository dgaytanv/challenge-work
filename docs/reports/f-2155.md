## What the figures show that the table does not

1. **The mask fix trades the wedge tail for everything else.** At wedge s=1.0 the anchor holds
   AUC 0.682; both mask-fix runs sit at 0.483 and 0.484 — *below chance*, i.e. anti-correlated —
   while gaining +0.043 at s=0.8. Net wedge area 0.7982 → 0.7961 / 0.7947, a small loss. The
   +0.017 mean_area of the mask fix is therefore earned entirely in rect/strip/towers/cells, and
   there is a real failure mode at full wedge severity that the family area averages away. The set
   encoders do not have it (0.72–0.76 at the same point).
2. **The current lead is not a lead.** d-deepsets-aug 0.8183 vs d-pma0-aug 0.8162 is a gap of
   0.0021, below E's 3σ probe floor of 0.0025 — and d-pma0-aug's own measured std is 0.0038, i.e.
   wider than that floor on its own. On rule 9 these two are tied and the ranking between them
   should not be used to pick an architecture. (F1 colours d-deepsets-aug blue only because it is
   top of `table.md`; the figure legend shows both numbers with their errors.)
3. **towers s=1.0 is a dead point.** `dropped_fraction` is 1.000 there for every run, so every arm
   is pinned at AUC 0.500 and one sixth of the towers area is a constant shared by all candidates.
   Not wrong, but the towers panel has five informative points, not six.
4. Six of seven bench JSONs predate `--probe_repeats` and have no error bars; they are plotted and
   labelled `(n=1)`, per the brief. Only d-pma0-aug carries repeats.
