## Two questions raised with the planner (not blocking)
1. **Curriculum reach.** `warmup_calls=2000`; at batch 256 that is ~282 train + ~32 val calls per epoch, so
   `s_max` only reaches 0.85 near **epoch 7 of 25** — ~28% of training runs at reduced severity, and patience-5
   early stopping could fire before full strength. Offered to disable the curriculum (or drop to ~600) on run 2.
2. **Where the symmetries land.** B's train mode rotates phi and reflects eta; in my loop those hit the
   **degraded view only**, so the consistency loss must deliver rotation/reflection invariance *and* dead-region
   invariance together. The grader never rotates. Alternative: apply the symmetry identically to both views.
   I lean toward keeping it (rotation invariance is physically exact and should regularise) but it is the
   planner's call.
