## L4 primary result (a): the selection criterion, measured directly

Per the 04:14 ruling, L4's primary result is criterion smoothness, not a 3-seed variance test.

| run | seeded val | mean \|Δ val loss\|, last 10 ep | new best / 25 | selected epoch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| l4-valseed s11 | yes | 0.002161 | 25/25 | 25 |
| l4-valseed s22 | yes | 0.001893 | 23/25 | 24 |
| l4-valseed s33 | yes | 0.001966 | 24/25 | 24 |
| l1-sincos s11 | no | 0.007445 | 17/25 | 22 |
| l2-dxysig s11 | no | 0.005787 | 17/25 | 24 |
| l3-ema s11 | no | 0.005497 | 18/25 | 24 |
| champion, campaign 1 | no | 0.006682 | 16/25 | 25 |

**The groups do not overlap on either metric.** Seeded max 0.002161 < unseeded min 0.005497; new-best 23–25
against 16–18. Exact permutation p = 1/35 = **0.029** one-sided. Mean ratio 3.17×.

Unseeded epoch-to-epoch movement (~0.0064) sits at the independently measured corruption noise (**0.0059**,
8 draws on frozen champion weights) — the criterion is largely reading its own draw. Seeded, it falls to
0.0020 and becomes near-monotone.

What makes this identify the *seed* rather than something arm-specific: the four unseeded runs are four
different arms, differing from each other in preprocessor and in weight averaging, and they all land in the
same band.

**Why this and not the 3-seed std** (F(2,2), recorded so nobody re-derives it): comparing two stds from 3
seeds each needs a **6.2×** ratio at α=0.05 (4.4× at α=0.10), and a 3-seed std has a 95% CI of [0.52s, 6.28s]
— the reference's 0.0030 is consistent with anything in [0.0016, 0.0189]. This measurement uses 7 runs × 25
epochs of paired within-run observations instead.

## L4 primary result (b): best-vs-last — mechanism verified, numbers pending

Verified structurally: on a **seeded** run best-val and last-epoch are the *same checkpoint* (same epoch,
encoder tensors identical). Only unseeded runs have a lottery to measure. Confirmed on l4-valseed s11
(best 25 = last 25, identical) against l1-sincos s11 (best 22, last 25, differ).

WP-H's references: s11 best 23 / last 24 (differ), s22 best 17 / last 22 (differ, early-stopped), **s33 best
24 = last 24 (identical)** — s33's gap is structurally zero and is excluded rather than averaged in.
s22-last is queued; s11-last and l1-s11-last were drained to free bench tokens and are on a deferred list.

## The bigger half, from WP-H: the same lottery sets the training budget

Early stopping reads the same criterion. WP-H's s22 stopped at epoch 23 after its epoch-17 best. Longest
non-improving streak: seeded 0/1/1 (margin 5/4/4 to the patience-5 threshold), unseeded 3/2/1/2 (margin
2/3/4/3), s22 reached 5. Unseeded runs sit two or three bad draws from losing budget; one did. Seeded runs
cannot early-stop while the criterion is monotone.

Reported as **mechanistic support, not an independent result** — the streak metric overlaps between groups
(l3-ema also reached 1) where the smoothness metric does not.

**Not additive** (WP-H): both effects read one criterion, so a bad streak both truncates the budget and leaves
the kept checkpoint older. s22 lost budget at the end *and* kept epoch 17. Best-vs-last and budget loss are
overlapping consequences of one draw.
