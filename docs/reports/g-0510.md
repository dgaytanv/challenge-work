# WP-G report 05:10 — G6 decided on numbers: **G6b. io_stream is impossible, and I have the exception to prove it**

Branch `wp-g` in `~/c2-g`, pushed. Sources: `~/hackathon-shared/quant/g6*.json`, `~/hackathon-shared/runs/g6a-*`.

## 1. The G6 decision

**G6a (io_stream) is not reachable in hls4ml 1.3.0 by any model-side change.** Campaign 1 recorded that
io_stream was blocked by *heterogeneous* activation quantization and would become legal if the quantizers
were forced homogeneous. **That is wrong, and I falsified it by running it rather than arguing it.** With
`build_model(homogeneous=True)` — every data-lane quantizer per-tensor — the conversion still raises:

```
NotImplementedError: Heterogenous quantization for activations is only supported with IOType=io_parallel
```

The message is misleading. `hls4ml/backends/fpga/passes/hgq_proxy_model.py::ProcessFixedPointQuantizerLayer.transform`
begins `if model.config.config['IOType'] != 'io_parallel': raise NotImplementedError(...)` — **the guard tests
IOType only and never inspects the quantizer's granularity**, and hls4ml inserts a `FixedPointQuantizer`
layer for *every* HGQ2 quantizer. So **every HGQ2 model is refused for io_stream**, homogeneous or not.
Two further independent blockers, each fatal on its own: `backends/vivado/passes/einsum.py:55` asserts
`io_type == 'io_parallel'` and our attention pooling is a `QEinsum`; and the io_stream softmax requires
`axis = -1` while ours reduces over axis 1, the token axis. Recorded in `quant/g6a_iostream_verdict.json`.

**G6b is the design.** Not by elimination — it works, it is cheap, and it preserves the measured accuracy by
construction (same weights, same arithmetic; §3).

## 2. G6a measured anyway — the homogeneity constraint costs nothing on the ruler
Worth having even though io_stream is dead: it prices the constraint for any future hls4ml that lifts the
guard, and it is the campaign-2-standard 3-seed number for the operating point, which campaign 1 never had.
6 QAT runs (3 seeds × {per-tensor, per-channel activations}), identical otherwise: 6-bit cap, ReLU, stage-A
seed, L1T train file, `--eta_max 3.0`. Benched on the L1T eval file, `--eta_max 3.0`,
`--train_data robust_tagging_train_data_small_l1t.pt`, `--probe_repeats 5`, `QuantizedPMAEncoder`.

| arm | per-seed mean_area (s11 / s22 / s33) | **mean ± seed std (n=3)** | probe std | EBOPs (N=200) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `g6a-het-*` per-channel (campaign-1 configuration) | 0.8071 / 0.8090 / 0.8096 | **0.8086 ± 0.0013** | 0.0004–0.0007 | **3.806e8** |
| `g6a-hom-*` per-tensor (what io_stream would need) | 0.8077 / 0.8089 / 0.8095 | **0.8087 ± 0.0009** | 0.0003–0.0004 | **3.981e8 (+4.6 %)** |

**Δ = +0.00009 against a seed-test threshold of 0.00274 → NOT SEPARABLE.**
So **per-channel activation bit widths buy resources, not accuracy, on this model**: forcing them
homogeneous costs +4.6 % EBOPs and nothing measurable on the bench.

The per-channel arm's 3-seed mean **0.8086 ± 0.0013 reproduces campaign 1's single-seed
`gF-bits6-relu-l1t` = 0.8089 ± 0.0005**, so the operating point now has a seed std, not just a probe std.

**A correction to what I told you at 04:36.** I said the two arms' outputs looked bit-identical. They are
not. The distillation metrics matched to four decimals because they are computed on the *calibration* sample,
where the per-channel ranges are never exceeded; on eval data they diverge, because the data-lane quantizers
use `WRAP` overflow, so a channel whose per-channel integer range is too narrow **wraps** where the per-tensor
(max-over-channels) range does not. The mechanism I gave was right about *why* homogeneous only widens; it was
wrong to conclude the arithmetic is identical everywhere. The 3-seed bench is the claim; the identity was a
hypothesis and it is retracted here rather than quietly dropped.

## 3. G6b — the fold, and the wall that was actually there
`--pf 1` folds the token loop; evidenced in the emitted `firmware/parameters.h`: all four token Denses become
`n_partitions = 400, n_pixels = 1` (one datapath, 400 iterations), and the attention einsum goes from 204,800
multipliers to `reuse_factor = 400, multiplier_limit = 512`. **C-simulation vs Keras at N=16:
max|d| = 0.000e+00, 100.00 % of elements exact.** Conversion at N=400 takes 6 s (campaign 1: never compiled).

**But folding the arithmetic was not enough, and the reason is not the arithmetic.** Vitis HLS on the folded
N=400 design reported, after 37 minutes of source analysis:

```
WARNING: [HLS 200-1995] There were 56,931,767 instructions in the design after the
'Compile/Link' phase of compilation.
```
(its own design-size warning threshold is 50 M). The design-size report says where they are — and it is not
the Denses, which the fold shrank to 29,888–257,888 instructions each:

| function | instructions |
|---|---|
| `phi_0`, `phi_1`, `score`, `v` (folded Denses) | 29,888 / 257,888 / 257,888 / 257,888 |
| **`nrm0_iq`, `phi_act0_iq`, `nrm1_iq`, `phi_act1_iq`, `score_iq` (quantizers)** | **7.76 M / 7.78 M / 7.78 M / 7.78 M / 6.15 M** |

`hls4ml/backends/fpga/passes/hgq_proxy_model.py::generate_mask_fn` **broadcasts the (k, b, i) masks to the
full tensor shape and emits one line of C++ per element** — 51,200 lines per `[400,128]` quantizer,
unconditionally, whether or not the mask varies. Six of those is ~300k lines of generated source.
**That is the real N=400 wall, and campaign 1's "io_parallel does not compile at N=400" was this, not the
multipliers.**

### The fix (patch P3), and it is exact
Our data-lane quantizers are homogeneous over `(batch, token)` by construction — they have to be, one
datapath processes every token — so every token repeats the same mask. P3 emits the mask once inside a loop
over tokens. It folds over the **largest leading block the mask is actually constant across** (the einsum's
`[400,8,4]` and `[400,8,16]` inputs vary over the head axis but not over tokens, so the naive last-axis split
would have missed them), it **verifies** that constancy rather than assuming it, and it falls back to
hls4ml's own code verbatim where it does not hold.

| | generated `nnet_code_gen.h` |
|---|---|
| hls4ml 1.3.0 as shipped, N=400 | **397,699 lines** |
| + P3 | **8,744 lines (45× smaller)** |

**Verified bit-exact: N=16 csim vs Keras max|d| = 0.000e+00, 100 % of elements.** Same arithmetic, smaller source.
