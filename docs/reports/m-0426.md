## Held-out was broken, and separately was measuring the wrong ellipse

**1. Unrunnable, 23:44 → 03:53.** `HeldoutDegradation.__init__` skips its parent's, and the
`eta_max` work added `self.eta_min` to the inherited `forward`. Every held-out run raised
`AttributeError`. That is promotion-rule item (b), so no candidate could be evaluated on it.
Fixed to the module ±5 grid — deliberately not a caller's `--eta_max`, because
`build_heldout_map()` paints on that fixed grid and taking a caller's value would silently
*mis-index* the map: a wrong number instead of a crash.

Regression, same checkpoint, `d-pma0-aug-meanpt-heldout_20260903_223745.json` (campaign 1) against
`m-heldout-regression_20260904_041255.json` (fixed code):

| family | campaign-1 | fixed | diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| ellipse | 0.7910 | 0.7912 | +0.0002 |
| annulus | 0.8120 | 0.8124 | +0.0005 |
| diagonal | 0.8033 | 0.8033 | +0.0000 |
| mean_area | 0.8021 | 0.8023 | +0.0002 |

The fix **restores** prior behaviour rather than changing it.

**2. Our `ellipse` was silently Group 3's.** `ellipse` names a family in both suites, from different
code. In `make_degradation` the colleague branch was tested first and matched the **bare** name,
and `--families heldout` expands to bare names. Ours drops 0.2311/0.3819/0.5780/0.7716/1.0000 of
candidates at s=0.2…1.0; theirs 0.0802/0.1589/0.2271/0.2871/0.3343. The retracted row recorded
theirs to four decimals.

Every field a consumer reads agreed with the wrong answer — tag, `families_arg`, the family key,
`eta_max_applies`. Only `dropped_fraction` disagreed, and nothing read it. Caught because the
planner noticed a seed cannot move one family by 0.036 and two by nothing.

Scope: **one row, mine**, retracted in place as `.RETRACTED` with a `.RETRACTION.txt`. All five
campaign-1 held-out rows predate the colleague module and are correct — audited individually.
