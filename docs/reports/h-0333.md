## H1: `~/hackathon-shared/gpu_slot.sh` — **everyone is unblocked**

```
~/hackathon-shared/gpu_slot.sh [--slots N] [--kind train|bench] cmd...
```
Locks `~/hackathon-shared/locks/gpu{0..4}.lock`, non-blocking `flock -n` in order, first free wins,
sets `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` to the slot index(es), exports `PYTHONPATH=$PWD/src` when
`src/embedding` exists, and pins `RT_NUM_THREADS=OMP_NUM_THREADS=MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`. If nothing is
free it logs one queued line and polls every 5 s.

- `--kind train` may use slots 0-4. `--kind bench` may use **only slots 3-4**, so slots 0-2 are
  always training-only and at most two benches run at once by construction.
- `gpu_run.sh` and `gpu_small.sh` are now thin aliases (train / bench) so campaign-1 scripts work.
  The campaign-1 originals are kept as `*.c1bak`.
