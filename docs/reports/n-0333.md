## 1. FINDING (new register class): the certified submission is the least-visible line in `curves_latest`

Everything about the accent is correct — and the figure still cannot be read for the thing it is for.

`plot_curves.py` draws rows in `rank()` order, which is **descending mean_area**, with no `zorder`.
Matplotlib draws later calls on top. So **the best rows are drawn first and are overdrawn by every row
below them.** Measured by counting exact-colour pixels in the shipped PNG:

| rank | tag | drawn colour | mean_area | px of its own colour |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | a-pma-rep20 | `#004488` | 0.8276 | **298** |
| 2 | a-pma | `#AA4499` | 0.8275 | 4041 |
| 3 | **d-pma0-aug-meanpt** (accent) | `#0072B2` | 0.8260 | **287** |
| 7 | d-pma0-aug | `#E69F00` | 0.8162 | 14340 |
| 11 | anchor-stock-baseline | `#D62728` | 0.7734 | 13538 |
| 12 | c-twoview-cos1 | `#666666` | 0.7712 | **21164** |

The certified submission is the **least visible line on the plot**, at 1.4% of the pixels of the
worst-performing row. The footer instructs the reader to find it by colour (`blue = certified submission`)
and it is 97% occluded. The four rows the ruling actually turns on (ranks 1–4, all within 0.0021) are
mutually hidden, because being close in score *is* being drawn on top of each other.

Nothing detects this: colours are unique, the accent is applied correctly (`#44AA99`, its registry
colour, has **0** pixels — confirming the accent overrides it), the legend is right, `check_layout` is
clean, the script exits 0.

**Defence:** set `zorder` from rank so the accented row and the leaders draw ON TOP, and make visibility a
rendered property that is checked, not a property assumed from having assigned a colour. I will verify the
fix by re-counting pixels, not by re-reading the code.
