## K5 is a null with an identified degenerate minimiser
The whitened (Mahalanobis) consistency term is `diff·cov⁻¹·diff` on the clean batch covariance.
A displacement becomes free if the model inflates variance along the direction it drifts in, and
the cheapest way to do that is to put all the variance in ONE direction. Seed 11, early-stopped
at epoch 6:

| ep | val AUC | ratio | cond | effrank | mse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.7216 | 244x | 787 | 1.18 | 0.45 |
| 3 | 0.7294 | 514x | 3470 | 1.03 | 104.9 |
| 6 | 0.7325 | 73x | 37820 | **1.00** | 1.10 |

Val AUC never leaves 0.73 against the champion's ~0.86 at the same epoch. The mse spike at epoch 3
is the covariance going near-singular before the model finds the escape. Seeds 22/33 not run
(planner's ruling): the failure is mechanistic, not seed noise.

**The diagnostic that caught it exists only because WP-I raised it against a different claim of
mine.** Effective rank was added as a guard on my "cond 6,000 vs 33" headline; without it, cond
37,820 reads as ill-conditioning and the rank-1 collapse is invisible. Registered with WP-N as a
class.
