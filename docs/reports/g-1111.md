## 2. Why, attributed rather than excused
The N=1 run is the control. At one token **P3 has nothing to fold**, so it falls back to hls4ml's own
generator and emits **byte-for-byte the same 2,689 lines** with and without it (checked against a
`WPG_NO_P3=1` build) — and that design sat in the same phase for 54 minutes. So the long phase is **neither
P3 nor the token count**. Once folded, the instruction count is dominated by **N-independent** parts:
383,350 at N=1 against 364,842 at N=16, chiefly the two 512-wide tail quantizers `norm_pooled_iq` and
`bottleneck_iq` at ~77.8 k LLVM instructions each, because hls4ml spends ~152 instructions per `ap_fixed`
conversion and the tail has 1,024 of them.

**So the next change is the tail quantization, and it has nothing to do with tokens:** quantize *before* the
flatten (so the quantizer sees a `[4, 128]` tensor with a foldable leading axis), or give the 512-wide tail a
per-tensor quantizer — up to ~512× less generated code. A **model** change, so the accuracy cost must be
measured; §8c.2's 3-seed tie for per-tensor activations suggests it is likely free. This is item 1 of the
writeup's "what does not work yet".

## 4. What WP-G did deliver
* **G6 decided on numbers: G6b.** io_stream is impossible in hls4ml 1.3.0 for **three independent** reasons,
  the first of which **falsifies campaign 1's stated diagnosis** — homogeneous activations do *not* unlock it,
  because the guard tests `IOType` only; plus the `QEinsum` io_parallel assert and the softmax axis
  requirement (`quant/g6a_iostream_verdict.json`).
* **The token loop folds**, through one existing `parallelization_factor`, no model change, evidenced in the
  emitted firmware: four Denses at `n_partitions=400, n_pixels=1`, the attention einsum from 204,800
  multipliers to `multiplier_limit=512`.
* **N=400 compiles and is bit-exact.** g++ **183 s** (campaign 1: killed at 2,710 s unfinished);
  **C-simulation vs Keras max&#124;d&#124; = 0.000e+00, 100 % of elements** at the full 400-candidate budget.
  Campaign 1's "buildability at 400 tokens is unproven" is closed on the numerical side.
* **The real N=400 wall found and fixed (P3).** hls4ml emits one line of C++ per element of every quantized
  tensor — 51,200 per `[400,128]` quantizer, 56,931,767 instructions after Compile/Link, over Vitis's own
  50 M threshold. P3 emits the mask once and loops, verifying rather than assuming the mask is token-constant:
  `nnet_code_gen.h` 397,699 → 8,744 lines, instructions 46× fewer, source analysis 9.5× faster, **bit-exact**.
* **G6a priced**: per-tensor activations cost **+4.6 % EBOPs and nothing on the ruler** — 0.8087 ± 0.0009 vs
  0.8086 ± 0.0013 over 3 seeds each, Δ = +0.00009 against a 0.00274 threshold, not separable. The
  per-channel arm's 3-seed mean reproduces campaign 1's single-seed operating point (0.8089), which now has a
  seed std for the first time.
* **The fit question answered on a proxy.** Instantiated multipliers, read from the emitted firmware:
  **107,776** at `pf=1` (146× the fold) and **34,048** in the recommended fitting configuration (462×),
  against 15,735,808 unfolded. LUT proxy 2.69–6.47 M (over) and 0.85–2.04 M (straddling) against the part's
  1,728,000; token pass 400 cycles = 2.00 µs and 1,600 cycles = 8.00 µs at 200 MHz. Both configurations are
  **bit-exact in C-simulation at N=400** and both are committed with their emitted `parameters.h`.
* **One claim retracted in place**: my 04:36 statement that the homogeneous and heterogeneous arms produce
  bit-identical outputs. They do not — the distillation metrics matched only on the calibration sample, and
  on eval data the `WRAP` overflow makes narrow per-channel ranges differ from the per-tensor maximum.
