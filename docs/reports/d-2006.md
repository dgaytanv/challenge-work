## Two things for the planner
1. **DeepSets full runs are dataloader-bound, not GPU-bound.** 563 steps/epoch x 8.7 ms = ~5 s of GPU per epoch,
   but an epoch takes ~90 s. Holding the exclusive `gpu_run.sh` lock for my Deep Sets runs therefore idles the
   A10 for ~95% of the time and blocks C/B/A. **Request: let WP-D runs go without the lock** (they use ~1-2 GB
   and almost no SM time), the way bench runs already do. I will keep PMA(L>=2) runs under the lock.
2. **Cosmetic, pre-existing, affects everyone:** train-set `AUC nan` in every epoch line is not a NaN in training.
   `ClassificationMetrics` gets fp16 probs under autocast; their rows sum to 0.9995, and sklearn's multiclass
   `roc_auc_score` rejects that with a `ValueError` which the stock code maps to `nan`. Validation (fp32) is fine.
   One-line fix (`.float()` before softmax) lives in `training.py`, which is WP-C's file — flagging, not touching.

## Also noted
The agreed WP-A placeholder degradation is a **weak** augmentation next to what the bench asks for: measured over
1024 events it zeroes 2.7% of candidates on average (p90 = 6%, 22% of events untouched), because one rectangle
covers at most 2.5 x 1.2 out of the 10 x 2pi plane (4.8%) and at most 3 are drawn. The bench families drop
17-75%. Fine as a comparability placeholder, but nobody should read a weak Phase-1 augmentation result as
"augmentation does not help" — that is WP-B's real generator's job.
