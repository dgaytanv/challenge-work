## 2. Future-work list, tail quantizers first (writeup §9)
1. **The N-independent tail quantizers**, the single most promising change. `norm_pooled_iq` and
   `bottleneck_iq` are ~77.8 k LLVM instructions each — hls4ml spends ~152 instructions per `ap_fixed`
   conversion and the 512-wide tail has 1,024 of them — and they dominate the phase Vitis is stuck in.
   Quantize *before* the flatten (so the quantizer sees a `[4, 128]` tensor with a foldable leading axis) or
   give the tail a per-tensor quantizer: up to ~512× less generated code. A **model** change, so the
   accuracy cost must be measured — though §8c.2's 3-seed tie for per-tensor activations suggests it is free.
